Albukhary International University (AIU)

About My University

Albukhary International University is a private, not-for-profit university located in Alor Setar, Kedah, Malaysia. It welcomes students from many different countries, creating a diverse and multicultural campus where students learn from each other's cultures and experiences.

My Programme

I am currently enrolled in the Foundation in Computing programme under the Center for Foundation and General Studies. This programme introduces the fundamentals of computing, including programming, mathematics, and web development, and prepares students for a bachelor's degree in a computing-related field.

My Learning Experience

My learning experience at AIU has been both challenging and rewarding. Through courses such as Introduction to Web Programming, I have gained practical skills by building real webpages. Studying with international classmates has also improved my communication and teamwork skills.
